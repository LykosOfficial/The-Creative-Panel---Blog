This policy explains what information {legal.siteName} ({legal.site}), run by {legal.owner} ("I", "me", "we"), collects when you visit, how it's used, and the choices you have. The short version: there are no accounts, nothing you do here is sold, and the only personal details stored are the ones you choose to give.

## What's collected

### Things you give me

- **Newsletter sign-ups.** Your email address, when you subscribe. It goes straight to EcoSend, the newsletter service, and isn't kept on this site's own servers. It's used only to send the newsletter.
- **Comments.** The name you enter, your comment, and (only if you add it) your email address. Your name and comment are shown publicly under the post. Your email is never shown or shared; it's only there so I can reach you about your comment.

### Things collected automatically

- **Rate limiting.** When you comment or like a post, your IP address is turned into a one-way, salted code (a "hash") so the site can block spam floods. The IP address itself is not stored, and the code can't be turned back into it.
- **Read and like counts.** Each post keeps a running total of views and likes. These totals aren't linked to you.
- **Settings saved in your browser.** Your light/dark choice and the posts you've liked or saved are kept in your own browser's local storage. They never leave your device, and clearing your browser data removes them. See the [Cookie Policy](/cookies).
- **Hosting logs.** The site is served by Cloudflare, which processes technical request data (like IP address and browser type) to deliver pages and protect against attacks.

## Analytics

The site uses **Google Tag Manager** and **Google Analytics** to understand how it's used: which pages are read, how visitors find the site, and roughly where they're from. Google collects this with cookies and device data (see the [Cookie Policy](/cookies)). I only see totals, not who you are. You can opt out with [Google's opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## Who your information is shared with

I don't sell or rent your personal information, and I don't share it for anyone else's marketing. It's only handled by the services that run the site:

| Service | What it does | What it receives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cloudflare | Hosting and security | Technical request data |
| Prisma Postgres | Database | Comments |
| EcoSend (by GoSquared) | Stores the newsletter list and sends the newsletter | Newsletter emails |
| Google AdSense | Advertising | Cookie and device data (see above) |
| Google Tag Manager / Analytics | Visit statistics | Cookie and device data (see above) |

I may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the site and its readers from fraud or abuse.

## How long it's kept

- **Newsletter emails:** until you unsubscribe. Every newsletter has an unsubscribe link, or you can email me.
- **Comments:** for as long as the post is online, unless you ask me to remove them.
- **Spam-protection codes:** kept with the comment they belong to.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you can ask to see the personal information I hold about you, correct it, delete it, or object to how it's used. You can also withdraw consent, such as unsubscribing, at any time. To make a request, contact me at <Contact />. I'll reply within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Where the GDPR applies, the legal bases are your **consent** (newsletter, personalized ads) and my **legitimate interest** in running a secure, spam-free site (comments, rate limiting, hosting logs).

## Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU/UK), and I don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has shared personal information here, contact me and I'll delete it.
